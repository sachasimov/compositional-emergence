# Provisional operational definitions

These definitions enable experiments; they are not universal definitions of complexity or individuality.

**Micro-dynamics:** `X_(t+1)=F(X_t)`, or a declared stochastic kernel, together with topology, boundary conditions and initialization.

**Macroscopic description:** `Z_t=phi(X_t)`. A possible information-closure criterion asks whether a microscopic past adds information about a future macrovariable given the macroscopic past: `I(X_past; Z_future | Z_past)`. Refer to Rosas (2026) for exact conditions and assumptions.

**Persistent localized structure:** For CE-001 only, a nonempty finite pattern on a quiescent background that exactly recurs after some number of steps, possibly under translation. This handles select still lifes, oscillators and spaceships but excludes many plausible objects.

**Candidate composition:** Following an observed encounter, a distinguishable product persists beyond a prespecified observation threshold. It is not automatically an increase in complexity.

**Compositional robustness:** Candidate measure of whether an isolated object’s effective macrodescription continues to predict its behavior when interacting with another object, with limited extra interaction variables such as relative phase and position. Its novelty remains to be checked.

**Cumulative construction:** Evidence of productive interactions in multiple generations, with verified ancestry and without engineered targets.

**Complexity:** Do not conflate Shannon entropy, algorithmic description length, entropy production, persistence, function, information closure, and hierarchical depth.
