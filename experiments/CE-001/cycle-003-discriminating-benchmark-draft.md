# Discriminating benchmark — draft, not frozen or executed
Goal: establish whether information/intervention-based detection adds value beyond geometry, before testing composition. Primary hypothesis: an information-based detector recovers independently annotated persistent supports better than geometric recurrence on a panel where their predictions differ. Null: gains disappear on held-out cases or after accounting for candidate selection and coding overhead. No claim of objective ontology.
Candidate panel: distant independently phased vs synchronized blinkers (common-cause control); near independent blocks; disconnected toad phase; isolated block vs transient singleton; moving glider and transformed phases; collision tracks with unknown product labels. Include empty and matched-density random controls; unknown cases excluded from supervised denominator, retained in logs. Design labels from analytic trajectories before detector runs. Separate development and held-out pattern families, transformations and contexts; do not count frames as independent units. Prior Cycle 002 cases are development only.
Methods: (1) geometric connected components and existing localized recurrence; (2) passive history MI as previous pilot; (3) response graph and recovery contrast as separate fixed methods; (4) local predictive-state baseline following Rupe/Crutchfield, with explicit lightcone horizons and training-only equivalence estimation; (5) candidate MDL predictor. No posthoc OR-combination or threshold selection on test data. Candidate supports, phases, motion and model choices must be encoded or charged consistently.
Prediction task: forecast identical held-out full local future patches including exterior halo, at specified horizons, from past only. Compare full patch predictor, occupancy, recurrence and candidate macro predictor under same training and compute budgets. Use prefix-compatible code lengths: model+support+latent state+residual, or explicitly label prequential proxy. Exact simulator is a reference with known rule and initial state, not a claim that macro prediction has more information. Report bits and wall time separately. Compare interventions only within a separately labeled experiment with the same intervention budget across competing methods.
Scoring: exact support/period/displacement matching where labels known; precision, recall, count errors, abstentions, false merger and split rates; held-out predictive log loss and net coding cost. Require improvement over strongest eligible baseline on held-out discriminating cases, not merely previous easy fixtures. Report per-case results and uncertainty by independent trajectory or fixture family. No claim of improvement if all methods tie, if static or synchronized controls fail, or if gains vanish after coding costs. No arbitrary numeric pass threshold until implementation and independent preflight freeze it.
Next gate: independent reviewer verifies prior-art method, labels, candidate generation, budgets, seeds, scoring and protocol; freeze exact panel and version before execution. No run in this draft. No Stage C or composition claim until detector validated. Later test H2 on held-out encounters with lifetime, abundance and encounter-rate controls; compare predictive autonomy under coupling as predictor of persistent products. No automatic merge.
