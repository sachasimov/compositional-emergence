# Cycle 003 — blind spatial partition pilot v1

Question: can a data-driven partition recover the independent dynamical regions in the previous Life pilot, without supplied object coordinates or size? This is exploratory, not an object detector or H4 confirmation. No independent review yet.

Use 15x15 periodic Life (B3/S23). Enumerate four equiprobable initial phase combinations of two blinkers at rows 5 and 5+d, column 7, d=3,4,5,6; rotate entire configurations by 90 degrees as a transformation control. Add single blinker (two equiprobable phases), empty, and two independent static 2x2 blocks (single deterministic initial state, negative identifiability control). Observe times 0,1,2,4. All fixtures known to experiment generator only; selection gets arrays of trajectories and no labels/centers. Compute each cell's four-time history across ensemble; keep cells with nonzero marginal history entropy. Form undirected graph of those cells with edge weight exact mutual information of histories. Components at thresholds 0.01,0.5,1.01 bits (inclusive) are candidate regions. Compare with geometric 8-connected components on same active-variable mask. For each threshold report full support coordinates, sizes, entropy of each component's joint history, and sum marginal component entropy minus joint entropy (total correlation). No optimization or threshold choice after results; all thresholds reported. Nonlocal MI can join disconnected regions; common cause can produce edges; finite phase ensemble and four frames are observer choices; no causal autonomy claim. Cells with constant history across ensemble are excluded even if they are active in time: explicitly report excluded ever-live cells, including static objects. Empty graph means abstain, not absence of individuals. No temporal tracking, model costs, spontaneous search or Stage C. Expected: d>=4 yields two components at thresholds <=0.5, d=3 may merge; rotated supports must be exact rotations of originals; empty/static controls abstain. 11 fixtures total (8 paired, single, empty, static), 33 threshold outcomes. Stop after fixed panel; keep every outcome. Run the exact committed function in JS isolate; standalone Node and pytest separate. No independent review claimed.
