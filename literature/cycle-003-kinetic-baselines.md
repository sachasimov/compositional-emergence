## Decision
Separate modular insulation, exact lumpability, functional integration and novelty. Independence can permit reusable components but zero coupling cannot itself generate cross-dependent functions. Conversely coupling can break insulation without generating a viable organization. No universal monotonic relationship follows. Strongest next test: compare prior-art reduction and retroactivity metrics with predictive interface descriptors on independently labeled integration outcomes, with identical targets and resource budgets. Ravoni and AlChemy reference implementations/methods first, independent preflight pending. No claim of novelty, no confirmatory run.
