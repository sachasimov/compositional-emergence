## Decision
Do not claim novelty for emergence of organizations composed of organizations: Fontana/Buss explicitly studied it. Prioritize full methods of their L1/L2 model alongside Rule 54 replication; compare two complementary model families rather than inventing more Life detectors. Distinguish novelty of types, production closure, organizational level, verified ancestry, and ongoing evolutionary novelty. Candidate residual question: does costed predictive interface sufficiency predict formation and reuse of higher-order organizations beyond reaction closure, self-maintenance, encounter opportunity and structural complexity? Not verified as novel. In finite closed CA, state trajectories eventually recur; do not infer open-endedness from finite novelty. Separate resource flows, environmental coupling and selection from fixed underlying law. No new run, no Stage C, no automatic merge.
