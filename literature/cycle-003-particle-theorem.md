## Research consequences
Separate reaction closure of a finite particle-type catalog from persistent composite formation and recursively reusable novel types. A finite closed catalog can support computation and repeated reactions without increasing type repertoire. Conversely a novel type need not be a composite. Distinguish event arity, novelty, persistence, and verified compositional ancestry; no claim of unbounded growth from finite data.
Use published Rule 54 collision tables and theorem as positive replication controls; use the pairwise closed catalog as a negative control for repertoire expansion, not a claim that Rule 54 cannot form hierarchy. Check multiparticle and composite products separately. Define outcome identity up to specified spacetime symmetries and background; report ambiguous products and finite-horizon censoring.
Proposed residual question: conditional on actual collision opportunities and established structural-complexity/phase bounds, do reusable predictive interfaces predict persistent new products with subsequent productive interactions? Competing null: collision arity, phase, velocity, lifetime and opportunity explain the outcomes. No novelty certification.
