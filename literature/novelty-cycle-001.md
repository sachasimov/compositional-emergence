## Assessment

Detection, particle interactions, autonomy, and causal multigeneration lineages
have substantial prior art [S2–S5, S8]. A contribution needs a narrower empirical
result and reproducible comparison. A candidate question is whether independently
measured robustness of macro-prediction under interaction improves **held-out
prediction of productive composition**, controlling simple covariates and
relevant prior methods. This review has not established that it is new.

## Falsifiable test proposals (not frozen protocols)

These proposals **do not authorize another cycle**. Sample sizes, rules, densities,
horizons, detector, estimator, and resource budget require a frozen approved
protocol before confirmatory runs. CE-001 must first validate identification:
whole-grid recurrence is not an interaction label. Designed configurations can
validate methods but cannot count as spontaneous discoveries.

Prospective endpoint: a tracked encounter yields a distinguishable product type
different from each incoming type, persists for a fixed post-encounter horizon,
and has auditable ancestry. Report per-encounter rates and trajectories without
encounters. Multi-generation composition additionally requires products in later
qualifying encounters. Freeze type equivalence, horizon, and ancestry attribution
before labeling; preserve ambiguous events.

- **H1, scoped:** in a declared CA ensemble, lifetime alone fails to predict
  per-encounter product formation adequately. Compare a frozen lifetime-only
  predictor with lifetime + abundance + encounter exposure + phase/relative
  velocity + rule/boundary/density covariates. Fix adequacy tolerance and loss
  beforehand. H1 loses support if lifetime-only performance meets that tolerance
  throughout held-out strata. Distinct outcomes among equally persistent cases
  reject deterministic sufficiency but do not establish a useful alternative
  predictor. No products is a valid, potentially uninformative null outcome.
- **H2, scoped:** robustness of macro-prediction under coupling reduces held-out
  encounter-outcome log loss beyond that baseline. Measure robustness on separate
  calibration trajectories with a frozen macrovariable family, e.g. the loss
  increase from isolated to perturbed prediction; declare partner phase/offset
  inputs. Gain = baseline loss minus augmented loss. A prespecified positive
  minimum gain and a trajectory-clustered interval wholly above it supports the
  scoped hypothesis. Gain at/below zero or an interval ruling out the minimum is
  negative evidence; an interval spanning it is inconclusive. Do not select
  features using held-out outcomes. Match model capacity and include a shuffled
  robustness feature to detect incidental gains.
- **H3, scoped surrogate:** freeze one visual statistic and its required ordering
  or predictive-accuracy criterion for the endpoint. Reproducible ranking
  reversals beyond prespecified uncertainty tolerance falsify that surrogate
  under the tested conditions. Success supports local usefulness and must also
  be reported. Neither outcome settles a universal claim about visual complexity.

## Baselines and reproducibility

CE-001 method validation should compare recurrence with always-negative,
nonempty-occupancy, and simple persistence predictors on identical cases. Report
confusion counts and undefined precision. Include empty, extinguishing, clutter,
boundary-crossing, overlapping and collision cases. Perfect handpicked-pattern
scores do not validate a general detector. A later general detector additionally
needs applicable domain/particle or automatic-filter comparisons [S4–S5].

For future H1/H2 runs, preserve every sampled trajectory, including zero objects,
zero encounters, extinction, errors, and uncertain ancestry. Split by trajectory
and rule family rather than nearby frames; report clustered uncertainty,
grid/horizon sensitivity, per-rule outcomes, and unidentifiable covariates.
Record rule tables, initial-state provenance, boundaries, seeds, configurations,
protocol/metric/dependency versions, code revision and local changes, artifact
hashes, and exclusions. Supply rerun commands and machine-readable run indexes.
These are prospective acceptance requirements, not experiments claimed to exist.

## Negative evidence and unresolved objections

1. **Novelty objections:** S4/S5 preclude broad novelty in detecting particles or
   discussing autonomy; S8 precludes treating lineages or multiple generations
   as unexplored territory. Preserve these objections in the PR.
2. **Logical counterexample to an overclaim:** a constant macrovariable can be
   perfectly predictable without a localized interacting component (S2). Closure
   alone is not an object criterion. This is a control, not a new physical result.
3. **Selection confounding:** a fixed rule can have been found by directed search
   despite no reward during its downstream trajectory (S8). Report selection of
   rule, seed, and observations separately. A method control can fail the
   discovery-ensemble restriction.
4. **Search absence:** targeted queries yielded no verified exact H2 comparison.
   Terminology, ranking, access failures, and unsurveyed literatures prevent
   interpreting this as proof of a gap.
5. **Preserved verification failures:** final S1 text, final S8 text, S7 proof
   PDF, and Rupe/Crutchfield full text were not obtained. Balduzzi is an unresolved
   critic-supplied overlap lead. No authors were contacted, independent numerical
   replication was not done, and no claim is promoted to validated finding.
