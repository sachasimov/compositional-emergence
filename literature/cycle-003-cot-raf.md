# Chemical organization and RAF integration — literature checkpoint
Author: coordinating assistant; not independent. Primary abstracts and accessible HTML sections inspected; no full PDF audit or simulation. Queries: chemical organization theory closed self-maintaining hierarchy; RAF irreducible subRAF union; autocatalytic cooperation boundaries integration; adjacent possible autocatalytic networks.
- Dittrich/Speroni di Fenizio 2007 DOI 10.1007/s11538-006-9130-8: closed self-maintaining species sets, stationary-state relationship under specified dynamics. Centler et al. 2008 DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btn228, section 2: closure and stoichiometric flux feasibility, established algorithms. Structural feasibility does not guarantee dynamic persistence.
- Steel et al. 2020 DOI 10.1098/rsif.2020.0488 section 1.3: union of RAFs in a fixed catalytic reaction system is a RAF; unique maxRAF; irreducible RAFs, computational complexity. Union is not proof of functional integration. Hordijk 2023 arXiv:2303.01809 gives formal algorithm reference. RAF food generation and catalytic availability are structural conditions, not kinetic viability.
- Hordijk and Steel 2015 DOI 10.1186/s13322-014-0006-2, abstract and introduction https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4333308/: generalized catalysis and self-generated boundaries offer a mechanism for higher-level RAF sets. Direct prior overlap with boundary-based recursive individuation; no novelty claim.
- Hordijk and Steel, Autocatalytic sets and chemical organizations (2018), author PDF https://www.math.canterbury.ac.nz/~m.steel/Non_UC/files/research/hordijk_2018.pdf: explicit comparative framework; full PDF review pending.
- Heylighen, Beigi, Busseniers 2022 DOI 10.1016/j.biosystems.2022.104720, abstract: resilience via feedback, resource buffering, degeneracy and specialization in higher-order organizations. Hypothesized mechanism, not universal theorem.
- Autocatalytic Sets Arising in a Combinatorial Model of Chemical Evolution 2022 https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1729/12/11/1703 sections 2.2–2.3: RAF algorithm in adjacent-possible growing networks; prior overlap with exploration and expansion. Do not conflate constructed network growth with spontaneous fixed-rule dynamics.
## Concrete implication
Two disjoint RAFs on shared food are a RAF in union even with no cross-catalysis. Hence maxRAF membership, inclusion depth, coexistence and closure are insufficient evidence of new functional individuality. A cross-reaction alone is also insufficient: it may be redundant, parasitic, or destructive. Use existing maxRAF and COT algorithms as structural baselines, then dynamic intervention and lineage as separate criteria. No independent audit; novelty of predictive interface hypothesis unresolved.
