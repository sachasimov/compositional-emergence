## Decision
No novelty certification. Highest-priority theoretical work: reconstruct 2001 bound and its antecedent, check subsequent literature and distinguish potential distinct products from realized persistent, reusable products and multi-generation lineages. A bound on diversity is not a prediction of productivity. H2/H6 candidate residual question: does an independently learned, costed representation of interaction predict realized persistent products and later productive encounters beyond structural complexity, encounter geometry, lifetime, abundance and rate? Test against the strongest established structural-complexity baseline, not only geometry. Possible tradeoff: removing phase information reduces prediction cost but may reduce potential outcome diversity; this is a hypothesis, not established. Rule 54 replication before new Life variants. Need full primary texts, citations to theorem/sections, independent review and frozen protocol. No claim that residual question is unanswered. Preserve prior pilot closure and Stage B gate.
