## Question
Does forward partial-event closure on the archived fixtures correspond to realizable ordinary CA histories, or could an implication be vacuous due to inconsistent inputs? For each pair A,B of situations, test existence of a trajectory satisfying A, A and B, and A but not B. Equal cursor displacement remains a separate requirement. This full-trajectory implication is not in general equivalent to Redeker's syntactic closure, which propagates pointwise facts without retaining all correlations. No claim of equality outside these fixtures.
## Method
Take earliest time among A,B, form finite union of all backward radius-one cones down to that time. Encode deterministic truth table with eight CNF clauses per noninitial cell and assert the indicated events. No periodic or fixed exterior values; every earliest row can be extended arbitrarily outside the required cone. Solve three queries per fixture by bounded DPLL with unit propagation (10000 nodes per query; limit is explicitly inconclusive). Verify every SAT witness by forward evolution of its earliest row. Separately enumerate every earliest-row completion consistent with the explicit initial events of A, using the Wolfram integer bit rule instead of the boolean rule used in CNF. Enumeration cap 16 free bits per case; no skip in this dataset. Initial interval is derived from the cone verifier's witness, so geometry and transcription are shared dependencies; neither is an independent audit. Enumerated assignments are finite artificial histories, not empirical samples. No trajectory frequencies inferred.
## Results
45 archived cases, 135 SAT queries, max 16 recursive nodes, zero limits. All 45 inputs have actual compatible histories. The 37 successful printed/reflected/parameterized reference cases and all three candidate corrections have A AND B satisfiable and A AND NOT B unsatisfiable, with equal cursor displacement. The three failing printed expressions, one retained transcription error and one deliberate negative control have A AND B unsatisfiable and A AND NOT B satisfiable. The three printed failures also have unequal displacement. Thus these failures concern actual CA event assignments as well as notation, within the shared transcription. Direct enumeration: 938 earliest-row assignments across 45 fixtures, 45/45 agreement with all three SAT statuses, no omissions. For 39a, 40b-1, 41d-2, respectively 8, 8, 128 compatible initial rows, none satisfying printed RHS. These counts include arbitrary exterior-cone bits and are not probabilities of natural collisions. All witnesses and counts preserved. Candidate repairs not author-confirmed.
