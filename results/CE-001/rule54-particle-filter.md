## Interface and individuality consequences
Phase variables and spacings are constrained by shared microscopic domain: only 1/8 nominal phase-gap combinations here are valid (18/144), for each of the five classes separately. These engineered cases are not random draws. The paper reports the outcome types are insensitive to relative internal phase for its pairwise collisions; our scoped check agrees where configurations exist. Distinguish local interface compatibility (admissible states) from the scattering map on those states. In this finite fixed-gap panel, ordered pair type alone predicts the final ordered product types, but this is a reference catalogue, not evidence that compression produces generativity. Predicting full trajectories, timing and positions needs additional state: alpha phases 444 vs 888 both map to AAA with identical domain surroundings; next macro rows differ (A0A0A vs AAA, position shift). Similarly beta 44 vs 88 map BB and have differing next macro rows. Complete one-row particle-type description is not deterministic even on isolated particles in infinite typed-zero surroundings. Retaining phase words suffices for isolated dynamics here; no universal sufficiency established for interacting particles.
Overlap: beta 5001 recognized as one B but simultaneously contains isolated right/left candidates (5 and 1). Alpha 50401 likewise includes both candidates and central defect. Paper already discusses composite stationary particles; parser's longest-match preference is not evidence of unique individuality. Distinguish alternative decompositions of a single phase from genuinely independent interacting units. Symmetry reflected pair classes agree in outcome type; full spatial/phase-shift reflection not evaluated here. Scale: local typed alphabet vs phase-labelled particle tokens vs type-only outcome; information savings depend on target, prior and cost of support/model/context. Causality: no ablation or spontaneous lineage, no kinetic inference. Scope: reference checks, not independent review or H2/H6 evidence.
