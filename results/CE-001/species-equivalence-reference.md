# Species equivalence on the Ravoni shared-food reference
2026-11-24; coordinator; same-author method preflight. Parent 0439863164c7e7e35f69872123539c4b3bfb883d. No independent review, confirmatory execution or Stage C.

Primary source: Cardelli et al., Exact maximal reduction of stochastic reaction networks by species lumping, Bioinformatics 37(15), 2175–2182, DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btab081; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.03342 v1, PDF pages 2–3 (Eq.1 and Fig.1), parsed text and page screenshots checked. Their criterion compares reaction rate sums over lifted product multiset blocks for each within-block species substitution and all reagent contexts occurring on either side. The diagonal is negative total outgoing rate; comparing only off-source product blocks is equivalent because each complete row sums to zero. The published result characterizes ordinary lumpability for every initial state, not just a particular trajectory. Existing ERODE implementation supports reduceSE with USER prepartition (https://www.erode.eu/docs/ERODE-manual.pdf, indexed manual text); not executed or version pinned here. Our deliberately small exhaustive oracle is not the published scalable algorithm or an independent implementation audit.

## Model and check
Reuse the eight transcribed reactions from ravoni-raf-reference.js, duplicate with shared food A,B,D,F,I,M, private C,E,G,H,L,N,O,P. Represent each spontaneous reaction and its catalyst-preserving counterpart separately (32 transitions). Use symbolic coefficient counts for common spontaneous and catalytic rate constants at unit volume; distinguish distinct parameters rather than floating-point arithmetic. Food singleton blocks; only corresponding private pairs may be joined, giving 256 partitions. No cross-species groupings tested. Inflows/outflows omitted; equal first-order private outflows and food-only inflows/outflows preserve the accepted G pair equivalence, but other environmental interventions need separate analysis. No kinetic parameter provenance claim.

For every joined pair s,t enumerate all reagent multisets after removing one occurrence of either s or t, compare reaction coefficient vectors for every off-source aggregate product block. Absence of a product block means zero. Sorted multisets retain multiplicities. Fig.1 positive control (S2,S3 with shared return rate k3) passes; breaking shared rate symbol rejects it (a structural parameter-independent rejection, not proof at coincident numerical rates).

## Result
256 partitions, 2688 substitution-context checks, only masks 0 and 4 pass. Thus the maximal parameter-independent SE refining the chosen pair partition has 21 blocks for 22 species: G1,G2 only, terminal products with no reactant or catalyst role. All 254 failed partitions retain their first coefficient witness and test count in JSON. This is a stronger scoped conclusion than the previous E/N witness for full aggregation. It is not a global maximal partition across arbitrary species equivalences, nor a claim about restricted reachable markings or approximate/weak lumping. The all-pair partition fails even with symmetric rates: correlated substrate and catalyst location is lost by summing. Whole-copy orbit lumping still holds under equivariant generator; it is a state equivalence, not a species-total equivalence. Labels may be removed without eliminating internal relational information. The accepted terminal aggregation is not evidence of productive composition.
