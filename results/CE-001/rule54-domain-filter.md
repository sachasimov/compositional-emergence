## Exact local closure
For a raw six-bit word abcdef, the three adjacent filtered symbols are T(abcd),T(bcde),T(cdef). The next central filtered symbol is T(phi(abc),phi(bcd),phi(cde),phi(def)), where phi is the Rule54 radius-one map. Enumerate all 64 six-bit words and group by the three-symbol input. All 57 admissible triples yield a unique next central symbol. For any infinite row or periodic ring the same local argument applies. The sevenfold ambiguity is confined to the all-zero triple, whose eight compatible raw six-bit words all produce output zero. Alternatively, any nonzero label gives four exact bits and the adjoining labels determine neighboring bits by parity; all-zero input is part of a periodic background whose evolution remains background. Extend the lookup arbitrarily (e.g. zero) on the 672 inadmissible triples out of 9^3; this defines an ordinary radius-one CA on the filtered alphabet agreeing on realizable rows. Radius zero is insufficient: raw 000000 and 000001 give filtered 444 and 440, both center 4, but next center 4 and 0 respectively. This answers the specific existence question in section 4.3 for our alignment/transcription; not an asserted new result in the literature.

## Compression is not equivalent to closure
For a fixed ring length and any distribution over raw rows X, typed filter Z=T(X) has H(X|Z) <= 3 P(Z=all zeros), in bits, since every nonzero fiber is singleton and the pure domain has at most eight preimages. For uniformly sampled raw rows and N divisible by four, H(Z)=N-24/2^N; for N not divisible by four, the filter is injective and H(Z)=N. Thus even exact radius-one macroclosure can be essentially a lossless change of coordinates rather than reduction of global state entropy. Sparse descriptions on restricted physical ensembles may still yield useful coding or computational gains; none measured here. Dropping defect types is not globally dynamically closed: on N=8 the raw rows 00010111 and 00100100 both yield binary defect mask 01000100, but after one step masks become 00100010 and 11101110 respectively. No deterministic map of the entire current mask alone can distinguish them, even with unlimited spatial radius. This is an exact finite counterexample to sufficiency of this binary abstraction, not to all history-based or context-dependent compression.
## Implications and limits
H2/H6 need a predictor based on genuine reduction of model, interface or predictive coding cost, not deterministic closure of an invertible relabeling. Preserve typed and binary filters as distinct baselines. Translation is handled by cyclic indexing; reflection tested with label permutation 1<->5, 2<->6 and fixed 0,3,4,7,8 plus three-site shift induced by trailing alignment. Observational equivalence is not causal autonomy; no intervention or lineage measured. Finite sizes test implementation, not sampling statistics; the 64-word local argument establishes radius-one closure for all realizable configurations.
