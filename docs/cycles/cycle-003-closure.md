## Formal closure of pilot 7
For deterministic Y=f(X,E), Z=g(X,E), if an interface W=h(X,E) gives H(Y|Z,W,E)=0, then H(W|Z,E)>=H(Y|Z,E), by conditional mutual information. Equality can be achieved by choosing W=Y, but that may be computationally circular and is not an admissible predictive interface unless independently computable within budget. Thus pilot 7 achieves the target entropy lower bound in its engineered ensemble, not evidence of a new compression law. A stronger test requires a single reusable W predicting multiple future targets across unseen contexts, with model/support/compute costs and no use of future output. Locality also gives a known finite-horizon boundary/lightcone bound in CA; boundary scaling alone is not novel. Need distinguish beyond-trivial-lightcone gains and link to compositional outcomes.
