# Scientific status report — cycles 001–003

Report date: 2026-11-19. Retrospective on experiments and reviews completed on
2026-11-18. This corrects the earlier chat handoffs, which emphasized PRs and
tests without sufficiently explaining findings or implications. No new
experiment or research cycle is part of this reporting correction.

## Where the research stands

We have improved a reproducible measurement method for selected persistent
patterns in cellular automata. We have **not tested the central hypothesis**
that robustness of macroscopic prediction under interaction helps predict
productive composition. No new scientific discovery or novelty claim has been
established. We remain in CE-001 Stage B method validation; spontaneous
discovery, collision-product ancestry and multigeneration composition have not
been evaluated by our experiments.

The important methodological result is that better recognition across phases
does not resolve object identity. In our fixed two-tub example, each supplied
part and their union are stationary. Every tested method returns one recurrent
composite where the benchmark declares two objects. This is a false positive
and two misses under that convention, not proof of a uniquely correct physical
partition or of an impossibility theorem about all possible identity criteria.

## Cycle 001 — baseline correctness and prior-art limits

**Question:** can we trust the local update rules and a narrow recurrence assay,
and how much of the proposed research already has prior art?

**Observed:** analytic CA checks and the recurrence pilot reproduced. On 11
scored engineered cases, the assay classified all 11 correctly, versus 7 for
nonempty occupancy and 6 for one-step stationarity. Eight additional diagnostic
cases were retained. The global assay failed to represent a stationary block
and moving glider as separate objects. It compares a whole grid with one
translated copy, so separate motions defeat that representation.

**Meaning:** the baseline is useful controlled infrastructure, not a general
object detector. Selected known-pattern success does not establish performance
on spontaneous trajectories or interactions.

**Literature result:** particle detection, collisions, autonomy frameworks and
some multigeneration lineages have substantial existing literature. Broad
firstness claims are unsupported. A narrower possible contribution is whether
coupling-robust macro-prediction adds held-out predictive value beyond lifetime,
abundance and encounter rate; its novelty and empirical validity remain open.

[novelty dossier](../../literature/novelty-cycle-001.md),

## Cycle 002 — local recognition and segmentation failures

**Question:** can we identify several localized persistent candidates when the
whole grid does not recur under a single translation?

**Observed:** the local method matched 13 of 16 supplied objects across 14 scored
trajectories, with no unmatched predictions. The whole-grid proxy matched 7 of
16, with one unmatched prediction. Six further diagnostic trajectories remained
unlabelled and unscored. The local method recovered separated objects moving
differently, but missed two nearby independent blocks and a disconnected phase
of the period-two toad oscillator.

**Meaning:** local recognition adds capability beyond whole-grid recurrence on
these fixtures. However, the answer depends on proximity and observation phase.
For the intended inventory task, recognition should be consistent across phases;
the observed miss exposes a detector convention, not evidence of a changed
physical identity.

Evidence: [methods and complete outcomes](../../results/CE-001/cycle-002-localized-v1-report.md),
[independent audit](../reviews/cycle-002.md). The baseline proxies are deliberately
weak inventory adapters; these results are not superiority over prior research.

## Cycle 003 — phase repair with a remaining identity ambiguity

**Question:** can temporal association repair the known proximity/phase misses,
and what errors remain under additional prespecified checks?

**Observed:** linking components across successive frames recovered all three
known misses. On the same extended panel of 29 scored trajectories with 38
supplied objects:

| Method | Correct matches | Unmatched predictions | Missed supplied objects |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revised temporal-overlap method | 36 | 1 | 2 |
| Previous local method | 25 | 1 | 13 |
| Same method with split/merge association disabled | 30 | 1 | 8 |

The association-only comparison supports a narrow role for temporal split/merge
handling on these phase fixtures. Nine diagnostics remained unlabelled, with
all dispositions retained and no supervised scores assigned.

**Negative result:** all methods fused the two supplied tubs into one composite.
The revised method therefore improves recognition without settling the
decomposition question. Its whole-window geometric links are not causal links;
it also remains limited by boundaries, recurrence bounds and motions without
shared live-cell coordinates between successive frames.

Old cases were development regressions. New checks were fixed before results
but authored with known labels, not blinded or independently sampled. The
cycle-002 score of 13/16 and cycle-003 score of 36/38 use different panels and
must not be presented as a direct improvement curve. The valid same-panel
comparison is 36 versus 25 correct matches in the table above.

Evidence: [submitted methods and case-level report](https://github.com/sachasimov/compositional-emergence/blob/de7a6d3bd2636c69703ff62c6b2e6bb37e39f2d2/results/CE-001/cycle-003-topology-v1-report.md),
[independent audit](../reviews/cycle-003.md),

## What is established, and what is not

- **Established under these protocols:** deterministic implementation checks,
  reproducible selected-panel outcomes, and the reported same-input method
  differences. The reviewer reran the same detector in another checkout and
  independently recomputed matching; this is not an independent detector design.
- **Interpretation:** persistent geometric recurrence alone does not select a
  unique inventory in the tested two-tub example. Further identity criteria are
  needed for the intended research claims. This is a methodological lesson with
  prior-art overlap, not a newly established general law.
- **Still untested:** H1–H3, predictive robustness under coupling, compositional
  productivity, spontaneous multigeneration formation, generalization to unseen
  rule families. Scientific novelty also remains unconfirmed. We have no
  experimental answer to the central compositional-emergence question yet.

Source verification also has limits: several inspected texts are version-pinned
preprints, Rosas's 2026 final proofs were not obtained, and prior algorithms and
numerical results were not independently replicated. Claim locators and source
hashes identify what was inspected; they do not eliminate those limitations.

Primary methodological context includes Hanson and Crutchfield's discussion of
phases and subparticle interpretations (§5.1 of their
[1997 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-2789(96)00259-X)) and Rupe and
Crutchfield's distinction between predictive-state construction and direct
causal relationships (§II B1 of the inspected
[2018 preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00515v1)). The linked ledgers and audits
record the exact inspected versions and limits of these source claims.

## Recommendation and pending decisions

My recommendation is to define an explicit component-identity criterion and an
independently reviewed labelling rule before expanding detector performance
claims. Use the tub ambiguity to state a falsifiable separation between mere
recurrence, separable components and interaction; then freeze a validation plan
that can fail. This is a proposal, not an executed new cycle or an assumption
that an intervention criterion will work.

The current research work is mostly infrastructure and method development.
Three cycles on that foundation should have been made explicit before asking
for continuation. A future report must make this allocation and its scientific
payoff visible, including a recommendation to pause or change direction.
