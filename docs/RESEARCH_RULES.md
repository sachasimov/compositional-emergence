# Scientific quality gates

Before: preregister rule family, seed strategy, boundary conditions, observation duration, metric definitions, positive and negative controls, and stop rules.

During: record code revision, dependency versions, all parameters, random seeds, exceptions, and data paths.

After: disclose every sampled rule, seed, success and failure; report uncertainty and potential confounders. Distinguish simple recurrence from interaction and multi-generation composition.

Before scientific claims: check the closest prior work, seek counterexamples, replicate independently, and state scope. Results from automata alone cannot establish claims about atoms or molecules.
